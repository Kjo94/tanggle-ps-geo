Frequently asked questions

Why does the same procedure cost differently at each clinic?

Cost depends on the surgical scope, the anesthesia used, whether an overnight stay is needed, and whether procedures are combined. Two clinics may use the same procedure name for different actual scopes, so compare written quotes.

How long does recovery usually take?

It varies with the extent of the procedure and the patient's own healing. Ask for an expected schedule at consultation and adjust it as recovery progresses.

What should I verify before choosing a clinic?

Whether the surgeon is board-certified in plastic surgery, whether the doctor who consults is the one who operates, and whether pre- and post-operative care is structured. Review scores alone are not a sufficient basis.

Why is procedure information hard to compare?

Clinics use the same procedure name for different actual surgical scopes. That is why a single number rarely answers "how much" or "how long", and why search results tend to return marketing copy.

Each procedure here has its own page: the questions patients ask most often are used as headings, with the answer immediately underneath.

How is the information verified?

Clinic figures are copied from what the clinic itself publishes on its own website. Cost and recovery depend on the surgical scope, the anesthesia used and the patient's condition, so amounts and durations we cannot source are left out rather than estimated. Every page shows the date the source was last checked.

Where to verify

Clinic and doctor details can be checked directly at the public and professional bodies below. Treat the institution's own record as the final word.

  • Korean Society of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgeons — specialist directory — verify board certification by name
  • HIRA — hospital and pharmacy finder — licence status, listed departments, specialist counts
  • Korea Consumer Agency — consumer counselling — the route if a dispute arises

How long does recovery take?

It depends on the procedure, so we answer with a published institutional range where one exists. The UK NHS lists 2 to 5 hours of surgery and 4 to 6 weeks off work for a tummy tuck. Procedures without an incision limit daily life less, but change less and assume a course of sessions.

These are general ranges, not a figure for any clinic listed here. Each procedure page carries its own source and the date it was last checked.
